One absurd clause, at most 200 characters, hidden deep inside a terms-of-service agreement — entered in the [The Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## The line

> Section 12.4: in the event of a service outage, the provider shall compensate each affected user, within thirty (30) business days, with one (1) live goat.

155 characters. Deadpan legal boilerplate until the last words — one mechanism (outage compensation), one concrete absurd image, twist word last.

## How it works

`main.py` writes exactly that one line to `/output/entry.txt`, with no trailing newline, so the file's byte count is the line's character count. It validates the 1–200 character gate before writing.
